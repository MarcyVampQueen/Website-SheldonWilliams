The Movement Project System:
Built for the Everyday Athlete

At The Movement Project (TMP), our philosophy is simple: Move better, then move more. You can't expect your body to perform at its best without first training it to handle the real-world demands of life. Whether you're a parent wanting to keep up with your kids, a gardener building a dream backyard without debilitating back pain, a corporate professional tired of the daily aches that come from sitting at a desk, you deserve a body that works for you, not against you.

The coach behind the work

Start where you stand.

Movement has been core to who I am for as long as I can remember. Ever since I first kicked a soccer ball as a kid, a passion for exercise has driven my journey. By the time I was a teenager, that initial passion turned into an appetite for variety—if an activity meant moving, competing, and hanging out with friends, I was all in. My schedule was a blur of basketball, ultimate frisbee, track and field, long-distance running, and endless games of tag. That endless energy and competitive drive set the stage for everything that followed.

That same drive carried into my youth outside of sports. As an Eagle Scout, I grounded myself in strong values, leadership, and a commitment to serving others. Later, serving in the U.S. Navy instilled a deep sense of discipline, rock-solid routines, and the mental toughness needed to persevere through tough times. Taking that desire to help people further, I became a certified laboratory technician. Working one-on-one with patients taught me true empathy, clear communication, and the art of personalized care. Every single step—from the youth sports fields to military service and healthcare—guided me toward my true calling: helping others transform their lives through fitness.

After graduating college with a Bachelors of Arts in exercise science from Concordia University Portland, I became a personal trainer and made it a mission to help as many people as I could to physically become the best version of themselves… to be continued
