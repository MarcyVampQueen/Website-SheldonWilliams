The Movement Project System:
Built for the Everyday Athlete

At The Movement Project (TMP), our philosophy is simple: Move better, then move more. You can't expect your body to perform at its best without first training it to handle the real-world demands of life. Whether you're a parent wanting to keep up with your kids, a gardener building a dream backyard without debilitating back pain, a corporate professional tired of the daily aches that come from sitting at a desk, you deserve a body that works for you, not against you.

The Movement Project / Services

Train for your terrain.

We may not all play under stadium lights, but being human makes us athletes in our own right. Life places heavy physical and mental demands on us every day, which is why TMP uses a progressive approach to establish structural symmetry, improve usable range of motion, and build lasting strength. By leveraging our MAP Protocol and the R5 System, we identify your unique vulnerabilities, teach you how your body was naturally designed to move, and build a custom program tailored to your goals.

We don't offer generic, cookie-cutter workouts. We build the physical capacity and life-sustaining habits you need to stay resilient, injury-free, and pain-free—because ultimately, the best ability is availability. Whether you used to be an athlete or have never stepped foot in a gym, our goal is to help you feel good, maximize your physical potential, and thrive in everything you love to do.

The coach behind the work

Start where you stand.

I've loved movement for as long as I can remember - from childhood sports to my time as an Eagle Scout and serving as a U.S. Navy Hospital Corpsman. But my journey hasn't been a straight line.

In 2013, I was overweight, struggling with anxiety, and hiding behind food and inactivity. When I finally decided to make a change, I lost 50 pounds, rediscovered my confidence, and fell in love with strength. Later, a severe knee injury sidelined me, forcing me to dive deep into movement science to figure out how to rebuild my body safely.

I know what it feels like to feel stuck, lose confidence, or fear that an old injury will keep you from doing what you love. I also know how transformational it is to pick yourself back up with the right support.
