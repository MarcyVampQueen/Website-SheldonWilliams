Class Offerings

The Movement Collective

Group coaching capped at 10 people. Two modes of training, one objective: to move better.

At The Movement Project, we believe training doesn’t just start and end with hard workouts. To truly maximize your potential, you have to train smart—giving your body what it needs and taking care of it from multiple angles. By pairing Durability with Recovery, we give you a balanced weekly rhythm that builds resilience without burning you out. Join us Monday through Thursday evenings at 6:00 PM at The Round in downtown Beaverton.

Monday & Wednesday · 6 PM

Durability

Strength and Movement

Get the attention of a 1-on-1 session with the energy of a small group. Limited to 10 people, each 55-minute workout is customized to your body and built around strength, cardio, and efficient, multi-directional movement. Your sessions also pair with targeted at-home routines designed to improve body function and overall range of motion—helping you feel good in your own body alongside great people who make the work fun. Move better, then move more.

Tuesday & Thursday · 6 PM

Recovery

Range and Reinforcement

Built as the ideal complement to Durability, this class is designed to give back what daily life and hard training take out. You’ll actively wake up underused muscles, articulate your joints to build a stronger mind-body connection, and expand your usable range of motion. You’ll walk out feeling noticeably lighter, looser, and confident that you made a positive change in how your body moves. Durability builds your capacity to handle whatever life throws at you; Recovery ensures your body stays open, connected, and ready for the next challenge. Together, they keep you moving better week after week.
